Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

KoreSignal ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information KoreSignal collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using KoreSignal, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We design KoreSignal to collect as little data as possible.

  • Data you provide: optional settings such as watchlist symbols and display preferences, stored locally on your device.
  • Diagnostics data (optional): if you choose to share crash reports or analytics, we receive anonymous diagnostic information such as app version, device model, OS version and crash stack traces.
  • Information from third-party market data providers: KoreSignal retrieves publicly available market data to display prices and signals. These requests do not identify you personally.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve KoreSignal's features and stability.
  • To respond to support requests that you submit to us.
  • To monitor usage trends and diagnose technical issues.

We do not use your data for behavioral advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Permissions

  • Network access: required to retrieve market data.
  • Camera / Photo Library (optional): used only when you choose to share a screenshot or attach an image to a support request. You may decline this permission and KoreSignal will continue to function.
  • Notifications (optional): used only if you opt in to price or signal alerts.

4. Third-Party Services

KoreSignal relies on third-party services to operate, including market data providers and (if enabled) analytics/crash reporting services. These services receive only the minimum data needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy terms.

5. Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall KoreSignal. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as needed to investigate and fix issues, and is then aggregated or deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

KoreSignal is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet, however, is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted in a future release of KoreSignal.
